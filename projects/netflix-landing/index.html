PROJECT 06

Netflix Landing

Streaming-service landing page and sign-in UI designed in Figma.

WebMotion
VIEW ON GITHUB VIEW IN FIGMA https://github.com/Ajayitachi/Netflix-Landing-Page.git

Overview

A UI design study of a streaming service's marketing landing page and sign-in page, designed in Figma. The landing page leads visitors from a bold poster-wall hero to a single sign-up action, supported by trending titles, reasons to join and an FAQ.

Key features

01

Hero with a poster collage, bold headline, price line and an email + "Get Started" call to action.

02

"Trending Now" row with numbered title cards and region / category filters.

03

"More reasons to join" cards with icons (TV, downloads, watch everywhere, kids profiles).

04

FAQ accordion with six common questions.

05

Repeated email call-to-action and a full footer with link columns and language selector.

06

Sign-in page: email or mobile, password, sign-in code option, remember me and sign-up link.

Challenges & solutions

CHALLENGE

Keeping text readable over a busy poster collage.

HOW I SOLVED IT

Darkened the collage with an overlay so the headline and call to action stand out.

CHALLENGE

Guiding visitors to one clear action.

HOW I SOLVED IT

Repeated the same email + Get Started call to action at the top and bottom of the page.

CHALLENGE

Organising a long page.

HOW I SOLVED IT

Split it into clear sections — hero, trending, reasons to join, FAQ, footer — with consistent spacing.
